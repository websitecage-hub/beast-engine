# Directives (replace weekly; read by the writer)

1. ARGUE, DON'T JUST DESCRIBE. Every reel must make one claim about the reader's own behaviour or about
   how other people misread them ("You didn't miss the call. You watched it ring."). The two April reels that
   got 35 and 33 shares both argued a point. Describing a mood earns a nod, not a send.
2. Do not open with "POV:" more than once in every five reels. Use these hook shapes in rotation: accusation
   ("You didn't X. You Y."), exact timeline ("The 10 minutes between..."), contrarian ("'Just be confident'
   is making it worse"), named audience ("If you reread a text five times..."), open loop ("Why you're
   fine one-on-one but silent in groups"), for-someone-else ("Read this before you call them rude").
3. One concrete image per line (a phone face-down, a drafted text, a timed laugh). No abstract feelings
   words without an object.
4. Write one line a reader would want to show someone else ("Quiet isn't always distance").
5. Never repeat a hook, a topic within 7 days, or the same reframe twice in a week.
6. Tone: tender, exact, never pitying, never promising. No cure/heal/permanent/guarantee/effortless/
   proven/trauma/journey. No advice. No "link in bio".
7. Close the last line so it echoes the first (a word from the hook) to invite a replay.
8. Prioritise these topics this week: group chats, phone calls, "I'm fine", cancelling plans, leaving
   without saying goodbye. Avoid: gym, dating, self-harm references.
