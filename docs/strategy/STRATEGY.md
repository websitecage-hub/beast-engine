# The bet

One format. A 16–20 second confession. One still. Lines arriving on the beat. No face, no AI voice, no "POV:", no comment word. The last line renames the verdict the viewer is already using on themselves.

That is the only format this stack can make that both travels and sells. Everything else either cannot be produced, gets a like and dies, or gets views from people who will never buy a $4 book.

The ebook is final. The Gumroad page is final. This file is only the marketing. The pipeline runs it. You do not.

## What actually distributes a Reel in 2026

Instagram's own ranking, per Adam Mosseri: watch time, likes, and sends, all measured per reach. Sends are the signal that pushes a Reel to people who do not follow you. Likes matter more with people who already follow you. Watch time is the gate. If the first test batch does not hold, the Reel never meets the people who would send it.

Three facts that decide the format:

- Most people watch with the sound off. If the words are not on the screen, the Reel does not exist for them.
- A card you can read in two seconds has no watch time to give. The algorithm reads that as a swipe, even when the sentence was good.
- Reposted, templated, and interchangeable content is suppressed as unoriginal. A grid where every cell is the same text card with a "POV:" label and a "comment MASK" line looks like a template farm. That is a distribution penalty, not a style.

Trial Reels — test on strangers, keep it off the grid, promote only if it holds — are the right tool and you cannot use them yet. Professional accounts need about 200 followers. Personal accounts need about 1,000. This account has 14. Every post is public. A dead post is not a free test. It teaches the account-level model that this page is low-value. The system skips a weak draft rather than publishing it.

There is no official length that wins. The rule that does win: the Reel is exactly as long as the idea. Padding a 16-second confession to 45 seconds to chase a "sweet spot" creates a swipe. A 17-second Reel watched nearly to the end beats a 45-second Reel left at a third.

## Why the current grid is stuck near 100

The strangers already arrived. ~100 reach means the first test batch was served and then distribution stopped. It is not a follower cap. Followers do not cause reach. Sends do.

Look at the two eras on the same account.

The April posts that got on the order of 68 shares did one job: they took a kinder name away and replaced it with the real one. "You called it introversion" is a sentence a person can forward without adding a caption. The forward is the point. The receiver is supposed to feel accused in a way that feels like relief.

The current grid does the opposite. Every cell is a full paragraph, readable at once, labeled POV, ending in "comment QUIET / HEARD / BLANK / MASK." That ending asks for a public performance. A like is cheap and public. A comment-keyword is a person agreeing to be seen wanting help. A send is private. The people who would buy this book live in private. The CTA selects for the wrong action, and the static card gives the test batch nothing to watch.

Same niche. Same account. The variable that moved was the sentence and the fact that it could be sent.

## What this viewer actually wants to hear

They are not scrolling for tips. They have already been told to breathe, make eye contact, and "just be themselves." That advice is the thing that made them feel broken, because they tried it and the body did not cooperate.

What they want, in the order they can actually use it:

1. Proof that someone has been in the specific room. Not "social events are hard." The phone, still lit, with a name they know. The half-second-late laugh. The text they typed and deleted. The aisle they turned down.
2. The verdict removed. The private sentence is never "I have social anxiety." It is "I am rude." "I am flaky." "I am fake." "I am cold." "I am too much." "I am broken." A Reel that says "you are not alone" is a poster. A Reel that says "you are not rude, your body treated the phone like a room it could not enter" is a correction they have been waiting to be allowed to believe.
3. Warmth that does not soften the fact. Confronting and warm means the scene is exact and the last line is on their side. Not clinical. Not a coach. Not a meme account winking at them.
4. No homework in the Reel. The practice is the book. The Reel's job is recognition. If the Reel teaches the release method, the $4 has no job. If the Reel only vibes, the $4 has no trust.

A send, for this person, is a confession they do not have to write. They forward it to one friend, or to themselves, because the caption they would have typed is already the last line. That is why specificity beats spread. A general Reel can be liked by anyone. Only a specific one gets handed to a specific person. The handoff is the distribution, and the person who receives it is the buyer.

High-arousal emotion gets shared when it also fits the sender's identity. Shame does not get shared. Recognition does. The line between them is the rename. Describe the shame and they feel watched. Rename the shame and they feel accompanied. Accompanied is what gets sent.

## The format

Name: the confession.

Length: 16–20 seconds. Not shorter. Not padded.

Picture: one Pinterest still. Slow push-in, so it is not a frozen card. The still must contain the object in line one. A phone Reel gets a phone, a dark bedside, a car at night. A random moody street under unrelated words is how a template looks. No faces that could be read as a stolen creator. No text already baked into the pin.

Words: 5 to 7 lines. They appear on the beat. They are not all on screen at second one. Center-safe, middle two-thirds, so the interface does not eat them. Large enough to read on a phone with the sound off.

Sound: original, and not a voice. This audience hears performance instantly. An AI narrator is another mask, and they will feel it before they can name it. The audio API is used for a quiet bed only — room tone, rain, a single phone vibration, a car indicator — unique per Reel so the audio counts as original. If the audio API can only speak, the Reel goes out silent. Silence with on-screen words beats a synthetic voice.

First line, inside 1.2 seconds: a concrete noun and a verb. "Your phone lights up." "You type it." "The invite sits there." Never "POV:". Never "social anxiety". Never a question. Never "nobody talks about".

Middle: the next two seconds of that same scene. One body fact. One time fact. No explanation. No second scene.

Last line: the rename. "You're not X. You're Y." X is the verdict. Y is the mechanism in plain words, not book jargon. No "comment". No "follow". No "link". No keyword. The last line must be forwardable with nothing added.

Caption: the last line, alone. No hashtag block. Hashtags are not what distributes this. A block of twenty tags is a bot costume.

One post a day, maximum, at 23:30 UTC. That is early evening in the US, late in the UK, and it is the hour this person is in the car or in bed replaying the day. Do not split-test the clock until the sentence is winning. Time is the smaller variable. After 30 posts, if the API returns when the audience is actually awake, the slot may move. Until then it is fixed.

## What the system will not make

Talking-head advice. There is no face, and a generated face would be both a trust kill and an originality problem.

List Reels ("5 signs you have social anxiety"). They get saves from people collecting identity, and almost no sends. One in ten, and only after the confession is already winning. Not before.

Memes, trending audio, celebrity formats, "tell me you're anxious without telling me." Those get views from people who do not have the problem. Views that do not send and do not visit the profile are a vanity the account cannot afford while every post is public.

Green-screen Reddit reads. Looks stolen, needs a face, and copying someone's confession is both unoriginal and cruel.

Comment-keyword CTAs. They train the wrong action. They also make every Reel end on a pitch, which is the pattern the April posts did not have.

Anything that names a diagnosis, a medication, or a method for self-harm. Recognition is allowed. Clinical claims are not. Crisis language mined from search is discarded, never turned into a hook.

## How a view becomes $4

The Reel never sells. The bio sells. The DM sells only after the page has been given away.

Bio, set once, then never touched:

For the person who says I'm fine.
The private version is in the book. $4.

If the token can write the bio, the pipeline does it once. If it cannot, that is the only line you type. After that the system does not ask you for anything.

Path:

1. They hold to the last line.
2. They send it, or they tap the profile because the sentence was too exact to be a content page.
3. The grid has to look like one mind, not a template. Same voice, varied scenes. If the grid is twelve versions of the same card, the profile visit dies.
4. The bio does not say "link in bio" inside the Reel, because the Reel already earned the tap. The bio states the price so the Gumroad page is not a surprise.
5. If they comment anyway, or they DM, the first reply is a page, not a link. The link goes out 18–24 hours later, one message, no follow-up sequence, no "just checking in." A second nudge feels like the mask they already live with.

Door posts — a Reel that mentions the book — stay off until the account has either 3 winning posts inside 14 days, or 500 followers. Whichever comes first. Then one door post in ten, and only in the winning shape. Selling on a page that has not yet been sent is how you spend the only trust the account will get.

The book's voice is warmer and more explanatory than the Reel. That is correct. The Reel is the private note. The book is the sit-down. Do not quote the book. Do not teach the release. Do not say "hidden program" or "RAS" in a Reel. Those are paid sentences. The Reel may point at the idea in plain words: it is not a personality, it is a filter. The how stays in the book.

## The mix, once it is allowed to vary

Until there are 3 wins, every post is a confession. No experiments.

After that:

- 70% confession. This is the product.
- 20% argument. One kinder name, removed. "You called it being introverted. You took the long way through the store." Same length, same rename.
- 10% door, and only after the gate above. One line, no pitch stack.

Mechanism posts are not their own slot. The rename is the mechanism. A separate "explainer" Reel is how the account starts sounding like a course.

## The quality bar

A draft ships only if a stranger could forward it to one friend with no added words, and three different people could each forward it to a different friend. Too vague and it is a poster. Too private — a specific jacket, a specific job, a specific city — and only one person can send it.

The gold set in PROMPTS.md is the bar. The critic rejects anything softer, vaguer, or more performed than that set.
