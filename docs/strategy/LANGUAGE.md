# Language bank

Seed scenes for the writer. The weekly miner adds to this. It does not replace it. A mined scene enters only if it has an object, an action, and a verdict, and only if it is not the same object-plus-verdict as a row already here.

Live Reddit could not be pulled from the environment that wrote this file. Do not treat these rows as quotations, and do not invent upvote counts. They are the recurring private scenes of this audience — the same scenes already visible on the account's own grid, and the ones that show up whenever this audience is allowed to speak without a coach in the room. The weekly search job exists so the bank does not freeze in this seed.

## How a row becomes a Reel

The writer is given one row. It must keep the object. It may not merge two rows. It must produce a new last line, not a swap of the sample rename. If the sample rename is the best sentence, the critic still fails a draft that repeats it. The sample is a target, not a script.

## The three-friend test

Before a row is eligible: three different people could each send it to a different friend. If it requires a particular job, city, relationship, or diagnosis, it is out. If a busy extrovert would also call it relatable, it is out.

## Seed

1. Phone, name you know, watched it ring out, waited ten minutes, typed "just saw this." Verdict: rude. Body: chest still tight. Time: the ten-minute wait.
2. Half-second-late laugh, nodded anyway, replayed the missing word at home. Verdict: slow, or stupid. Body: heat in the face. Time: the rest of the night.
3. Group chat, line typed and deleted, sent "haha" after the moment passed. Verdict: quiet, or boring. Body: thumb hovering. Time: until they changed the subject.
4. Said "I'm good" all day, hands started in the car. Verdict: fake. Body: hands. Time: the drive, not the room.
5. Cancelled, relief before the text sent, guilt an hour later. Verdict: flaky. Body: the drop in the stomach when the plan existed, the ease when it didn't. Time: the hour between relief and guilt.
6. Invite unread, wanted and dreaded, thumb won't open it. Verdict: ungrateful. Body: thumb. Time: four reads, no open.
7. Saw someone at the end of the aisle, turned off. Verdict: cold, or stuck-up. Body: the turn happens before the thought. Time: the whole drive home.
8. Recorded a voice note, hated the playback, deleted it, sent a text. Verdict: awkward. Body: the flinch at the sound of their own voice. Time: one listen.
9. Someone said their name across a room. Stomach dropped before they knew why. They smiled late. Verdict: weird. Body: stomach. Time: before the reason arrived.
10. Rehearsed a story, got to the room, said "not much." Verdict: boring. Body: the story still sitting in the throat. Time: the shower before, the silence after.
11. "We should hang out," said it, already knew they would not follow up. Verdict: fake friend. Body: the warmth was real and the follow-up was impossible. Time: the days after, watching their own silence.
12. Left on read, not from indifference. Opened the message, typed, left the app. Verdict: rude. Body: the app icon becoming a threat. Time: hours, then a too-late reply.
13. Photo of the group. They stood at the edge and still worried they ruined the frame. Verdict: too much, or vain for caring. Body: not knowing where to put the hands. Time: the second the camera came out.
14. Cashier asked a small question. They said the wrong short answer and replayed it in the parking lot. Verdict: awkward. Body: the rush to end the exchange. Time: a thirty-second talk, a twenty-minute replay.
15. Door of a party, already inside the building, could not make the last door. Turned around. Verdict: dramatic. Body: feet stopping. Time: one door.
16. Kindness from someone. Did not know what to do with their face. Said thanks too many times. Verdict: too much. Body: face won't land. Time: the thank-yous stacking.
17. Plans made by someone else, relief when the other person cancelled. Then shame at the relief. Verdict: bad friend. Body: the uninvited smile. Time: the moment the cancellation arrived.
18. Practiced "sorry I can't" for twenty minutes. The text was one sentence. Verdict: ridiculous. Body: the rehearsal longer than the event. Time: twenty minutes for eight words.
19. Walked a longer route so they would not pass someone they like. Verdict: cold. Body: the detour decided by the legs. Time: the extra block.
20. Funny in their head. Out loud, "yeah," twice. Verdict: dull. Body: the joke dying on the way out. Time: the gap where the joke should have been.
21. Meeting, knew the answer, waited too long, someone else said it. Nodded like they agreed. Verdict: useless. Body: throat closed on a sentence they had. Time: three seconds too late.
22. Got home from being "fine" and could not speak to the person they live with either. Verdict: broken. Body: the social battery story they use so they don't have to say fear. Time: the first ten minutes inside.
23. Blue ticks. Read the message, felt the obligation of being perceived reading it. Verdict: rude if they wait, exposed if they answer fast. Body: the seen-receipt as a small alarm. Time: the minutes after being seen.
24. Someone asked "you good?" and the true answer would have taken the rest of the night. They said yeah. Verdict: liar. Body: the true answer physically too long. Time: one second to lie, hours to regret the lie.
25. Laughed at a story they didn't fully hear because asking again felt like a debt. Verdict: fake. Body: the laugh as a door closing. Time: the rest of that conversation, slightly lost.
26. Wanted to leave, did not know the sentence for leaving, stayed until it was strange. Verdict: awkward. Body: standing when they had already left in their head. Time: the extra half hour.
27. A pause in the conversation. They filled it with a worse sentence than silence would have been. Verdict: annoying. Body: panic at the gap. Time: one pause.
28. Compliment. Deflected it so fast the other person looked embarrassed. Verdict: ungrateful, or fishing. Body: cannot hold being seen kindly. Time: the instant after the compliment.
29. Typed a real message to someone they miss. Deleted it. Sent nothing. Told themselves it was dignity. Verdict: cold. Body: the dignity was fear. Time: the draft that never existed for anyone else.
30. Woke up still in yesterday's conversation. Not a new day until the replay finished, and it did not finish. Verdict: obsessive. Body: the night didn't end. Time: before they were even upright.

## Families

The learner weights families, not individual rows, until a row has its own score.

- phone and messages: 1, 3, 8, 12, 18, 23, 29
- the body in the room: 2, 9, 14, 20, 21, 25, 27
- leaving and dodging: 5, 7, 15, 17, 19, 26
- the performance of fine: 4, 10, 11, 16, 22, 24, 28
- after, alone: 6, 13, 30

Start all five families equal. The mutation rules in ENGINE_SPEC.md move them. Do not hand-pick a favorite.

## What never enters the bank

- "Nobody understands me." No object.
- "Social situations are overwhelming." No action.
- Any tip, including tips that sound kind.
- Any scene whose only ending is despair with no rename possible. The rename is the product. A scene that can only be tragedy is not a Reel this account should touch.
- Crisis, self-harm, abuse, minors. Discard. Do not store "for later."
- A trend, a sound name, a celebrity, a meme template.

## Freshness

A row used once is ineligible for 40 posts. A family may recur. An object may appear twice in ten posts and then must rest. The miner may refresh a family's facts — a new time detail, a new body detail — without creating a duplicate row. Same object and same verdict is a duplicate even if the wording is pretty.

If the miner returns nothing usable for three weeks, do nothing dramatic. The seed is enough to run for months at one post a day. Do not loosen the three-friend test to fill a calendar.
